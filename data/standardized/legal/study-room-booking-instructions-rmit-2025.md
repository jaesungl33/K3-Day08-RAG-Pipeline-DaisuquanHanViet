BOOKING ROOM INSTRUCTION FOR USERS_UPDATE 2025

RULES AND POLICIES:

   •   1 ID can book a maximum of 2 slots at once
   •   Maximum time per 1 slot: 60 minutes
   •   Students can only book for 2 weeks in advance (14 days)
   •   The booking will be canceled 15 minutes after the scheduled time.


INSTRUCTION FOR USERS:

Step 1: Access to Library Website: https://www.rmit.edu.vn/libraryvn, choose “Book a study room”




Step 2: Choose your campus and sign-in

   •   Username: ID number
   •   Password: RMIT password




Step 3: Choose one room and Press “Request” then “Check availability”
